Privacy Policy

PDF Converter · Fast Image to PDF & Document Scanner

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

Summary: 100% Client-Side Privacy

Your privacy is our highest priority. PDF Converter operates entirely on your device. We do not upload, transmit, store, or view any of your images, camera scans, ID cards, or generated PDF files on any remote server. Everything happens strictly inside your local web browser.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Converter (“we”, “our”, or “the application”) handles your data when you use our web and mobile application. By accessing or using PDF Converter, you acknowledge the privacy practices described herein.

2. What Information We Process & How It Is Used

Unlike conventional online converters, PDF Converter uses modern client-side WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and JavaScript technologies to process documents locally. Here is what we process:

  • Selected Images & Documents: Any photos (JPG, PNG, WebP, etc.) you drag-and-drop or select from your gallery are loaded into your device’s local memory solely for compression, layout rendering, and PDF compilation. They are never uploaded to any cloud or external servers.
  • Camera Scans & ID Cards: When you use the live document scanner or 2-sided ID Card scanner, video stream frames are processed strictly in real time on your device hardware to generate composite PDF pages. No video feed or photos are sent over the internet.
  • Text Input: Text typed in the "Text to PDF" tool or watermark fields is compiled directly into the PDF binary on your machine.

3. Device Permissions

To provide optional document scanning capabilities, the application may request the following device permission:

  • Camera Access (Optional): Required only when you explicitly launch the "Scan with Camera" or "ID Card Scanner" features. The camera is used solely to capture documents directly onto the local canvas. You may deny this permission and still use all file upload and conversion features.

4. Local Storage & Conversion History

For your convenience, PDF Converter provides an optional Conversion History feature.

  • We store minimal file metadata (filename, page count, file size, and creation timestamp) in your browser’s local storage (localStorage).
  • This data remains stored exclusively on your device and is never synchronized to external accounts or databases.
  • You can delete individual history records or wipe all history at any time using the “Clear All” button inside the application.

5. No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising Cookies

We believe utilities should be clean and respectful. PDF Converter:

  • Does not use third-party analytics trackers (no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.).
  • Does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data.
  • Does not serve behavioral or targeted advertisements.

6. Security of Your Data

Because your files never leave your device, your sensitive financial statements, identification cards, medical records, and family photos are protected from data breaches, interception, or server-side leaks. Once you close your browser tab or clear your browser cache, transient document memory is automatically purged.

7. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

PDF Converter is a general utility that does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
